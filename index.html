DATABASE FUNDAMENTALS

A database helps us organize and manage information effectively.

They provide a structured way to store, manage, and retrieve data, making it easier to analyze and use for decision-making.

Why Choose A Database? They are essential for any modern business or organization that deals with large amounts of data.

Types of Databases:

  • Relational Databases
  • Non-Relational Databases

WHEN TO USE EACH TYPE?

When choosing a database type, consider the nature of your data and the requirements of your application. A relational database is best for structured data with a fixed schema, while a non-relational database is better for unstructured or semi-structured data.

Relational Databases:

  • MySQL
  • PostgreSQL
  • Oracle Database
  • MariaDB
  • SQLite

Non-Relational Databases:

  • MongoDB
  • Cassandra
  • Redis
  • Neo4j
